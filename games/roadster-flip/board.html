Starbase Run

Auto-drive at 300 mph across the Texas salt flats. Two buttons. One is a cold-gas fan that bends the air into a sonic loop. The other fires thrusters for the kickflip over Tesla Semis crossing the 4-way junctions — while Starships claw off the pad behind you.

The whole game

FAN ◀HOLD · SONIC LOOP
THRUST ▶TAP · KICKFLIP
  • The car never steers itself into a wall — it never needs you. It only needs you on beat.
  • Hold the fan on the cyan rails: the car rides a 360° (or 720°) loop on air.
  • Tap thrusters right before the junction: 4.5 m of hop and a full barrel roll over the trailer.
  • Let go mid-loop and you lose the grip. Miss the tap and you eat a semi. 3 shields, then done.
